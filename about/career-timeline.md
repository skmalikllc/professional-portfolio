## Reading the overlap

The two tracks overlap between 2015 and 2023, and that is intentional rather than an error.

**2003 – 2023** was the primary career: full-time technical and operational work, beginning with Pakistan Air Force service and running for approximately twenty years to August 2023.

**2015 – 2025** was part-time freelance work carried alongside it. Remote client services in digital administration, cloud systems, e-commerce support and workflow automation, built up over time outside primary working commitments.

**2025 – present** is full-time freelance work. The part-time track became the main occupation.

This is a gradual, evidenced career transition rather than two competing full-time histories. At no point before 2025 was freelancing full-time, and it is not presented as such anywhere in this portfolio.

---

## Why the transition happened the way it did

A twenty-year technical career gives you documentation habits and process discipline. What it does not give you is modern tooling. Building the second track slowly, alongside the first, is how the two ended up reinforcing each other: the automation work is more reliable because of the operational background, and the operational background stayed current because of the automation work.
