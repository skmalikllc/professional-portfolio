## My own Etsy shop — EverlanceAutomation

I operate my own Etsy shop, [EverlanceAutomation — Etsy Shop](https://www.etsy.com/shop/EverlanceAutomation), selling business-systems templates and setup services rather than physical products. It is public, which makes it the one piece of e-commerce evidence anyone can inspect directly.

Running it covers the full operational cycle first-hand:

- Listing creation and the structure of a storefront's sections
- Digital product packaging — assembling a downloadable product that works without ongoing support
- Gallery image design to a consistent visual standard
- Pricing structure and offer consolidation
- Matching listing settings and descriptions to the actual delivery method
- Small-budget advertising and reading the conversion data before changing anything

This is a working shop rather than a case study. **No revenue, order, traffic, conversion or advertising figures are published** — the shop demonstrates hands-on Etsy and e-commerce administration, not trading performance, and it is not presented as a large or high-volume operation.
