## What "working level" means here

Hands-on work in a maintained, inspected and documented environment — not a certification claim. Where an employer needs a formal trade qualification or a machine-specific ticket, that would be a separate requirement; what I hold is the Diploma in Machinist Technology (recognized as equivalent to the Diploma of Associate Engineer in Pakistan, as stated on the diploma) and the operational record.

## What I do not claim

CNC operation or programming, industrial machine maintenance or fault-finding, practical aircraft maintenance, CAD design, welding certification, hydraulics or pneumatics specialisation, or professional engineering status. If a role needs those, I would be learning them rather than bringing them.
