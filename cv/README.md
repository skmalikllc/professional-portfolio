## Rules all four follow

- One factual history. No version contains a claim another contradicts.
- No invented job titles, dates, employers or achievements.
- No rank, service number, unit, base or sensitive detail from the 2003–2023 career.
- Skill levels stated honestly — basics stay basics.
- No unverified figures. Where an outcome was not measured, it is described qualitatively.
- Contact details limited to a professional email; no home address, no national identity number, no date of birth unless a specific jurisdiction requires it on the application itself.

## Format

Each version will be published as a PDF, with a plain-text version alongside for applicant tracking systems that parse badly.
