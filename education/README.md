## Diploma in Machinist Technology

Awarded on 9 April 2005 by the School of Aeronautics, Pakistan Air Force, with an aggregate of **68.23%**. The diploma states that it is recognized as equivalent to the Diploma of Associate Engineer in Pakistan. That equivalence is as stated on the diploma, for Pakistan; no foreign or international equivalence has been established, and this is not presented as a bachelor's degree or as a US associate degree.

Academic training ran from August 2003 to June 2004, and trade training from July 2004 to April 2005. The diploma was completed after joining the Pakistan Air Force, through its training and education pathway — which is why the diploma years overlap the start of the service record. This period is initial technical education and training, not hands-on machinist employment; the machinist role is dated from April 2005 (see [experience](../experience/pakistan-air-force.md)).

Subjects included English, Aerodynamics, Physics, Technical Drawing, Material Science, Basic Electricity, Aircraft Technology, CNC Machines, Metallurgy, Lathe & Grinding Machines, Milling & Shaper Machines, Jig Boring & Slotting Machines, Maintenance Management, and Flight Safety & QC/QAP. Technical Drawing, CNC Machines and Maintenance Management were studied as coursework; they are academic knowledge, not hands-on CNC or maintenance experience.

The diploma is the formal qualification behind the mechanical and workshop skills listed in [mechanical & industrial skills](../skills/mechanical-industrial.md).

## Matriculation

Secondary education with a Science group, completed in **2002**.

**Institution:** Government High School Harnoli, Mianwali, Pakistan
**Examining board:** Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE), Sargodha

The Science group at Matriculation level is the entry route to the diploma, which is why the two follow directly on from each other.
