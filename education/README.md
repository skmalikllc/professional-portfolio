## DAE Mechanical

A three-year diploma in mechanical engineering technology, completed at the School of Aeronautics, Karachi, between 2003 and 2005.

The diploma was completed after joining the Pakistan Air Force, through its training and education pathway — which is why the diploma years and the start of the technical career overlap. The two are connected, not contradictory.

The DAE is the formal qualification behind the mechanical and workshop skills listed in [mechanical & industrial skills](../skills/mechanical-industrial.md).

## Matriculation

Secondary education with a Science group, completed in **2002**.

**Institution:** Government High School Harnoli, Mianwali, Pakistan
**Examining board:** Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE), Sargodha

The Science group at Matriculation level is the entry route to the DAE, which is why the two follow directly on from each other.

## What is deliberately not published here

Grades, roll numbers, certificate numbers, subject lists and accreditation details are not published, because they have not been documented in this portfolio. They are available to an employer directly on request.

Only the completion year is stated for the Matriculation, because that is what is confirmed. No start year is given, since it has not been verified.
