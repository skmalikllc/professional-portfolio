## Overview

Catalogue and store operations delivered as client work across Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Shopify and WooCommerce, plus a storefront of my own.

## Problem

Store operations is the work that does not get done because nobody wants to do it: listings that need rebuilding, inventory records that have drifted from reality, prices that were set once and never revisited, and images that fail the platform's requirements for reasons the error message does not explain.

It is unglamorous, it is high-volume, and it is where store performance quietly leaks.

## My role

Operations delivery — listings, catalogue, inventory, pricing, imagery and account housekeeping.

## Work performed

- Product listing creation and rebuilding, with attributes and categories assigned correctly
- Bulk catalogue work across platforms
- Inventory record maintenance and reconciliation between systems
- Pricing setup and maintenance across a catalogue
- Product image preparation to platform specification, and gallery structure
- WordPress and WooCommerce store operations and content maintenance
- My own Etsy storefront, [EverlanceAutomation](https://www.etsy.com/shop/EverlanceAutomation): listing creation, section structure, digital product packaging, consistent gallery design, and matching listing settings to the real delivery method

## Tools

Etsy · Shopify · WooCommerce · WordPress · Amazon · eBay · Printify · Canva · Photoshop · Google Sheets

## Solution

Treat a catalogue as a dataset rather than a series of pages. Consistent attributes, consistent naming, images to one standard, and a record of what changed — which is the same discipline as any other data work.

## Outcome

Delivered across the engagements recorded in the linked repositories. No revenue, conversion, traffic, order-count or ranking figures are published; my own shop's figures are a private business matter and client figures are not mine to publish.
